# 隐私说明 / Privacy

## 中文

绕线助手的图片裁切、绕线序列生成、TXT 处理、PDF 生成和项目存档均在设备本地完成。

- 应用不申请网络权限。
- 应用不包含广告、统计或追踪 SDK。
- 应用不会把图片、序列或 `.sar` 存档上传到作者服务器。
- Android 系统云备份已禁用。
- 用户主动点击 Bilibili、X、Telegram、邮箱或 `stringar.com` 时，链接会交给外部应用或浏览器；外部服务适用其各自的隐私规则。
- 用户主动分享 APK 或存档时，文件会交给用户从 Android 系统分享面板选择的应用。

## English

String Art Helper processes image crops, string sequences, TXT files, PDFs, and project saves entirely on the device.

- The app does not request Internet permission.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK.
- The app does not upload images, sequences, or `.sar` saves to the author's servers.
- Android cloud backup is disabled.
- When the user opens Bilibili, X, Telegram, email, or `stringar.com`, Android hands the link to an external app or browser. Those services have their own privacy practices.
- When the user shares an APK or save, the file is handed to the app selected by the user in Android's system share sheet.
